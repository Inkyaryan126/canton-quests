# Canton Quests — Live Game Director Operator Guide

> **Operational Handbook**: Practical step-by-step guide for a Game Master running a 60–90 minute live weekend event using the `/admin/live` Control Room.

---

## 🎯 Game Director Objectives
As a Live Game Director, your job is to keep Canton feeling alive, surprising, and dynamic. Players should feel:
**"Something could happen in Canton at any moment."**

---

## ⏱️ 60-Minute Live Demo Runbook

### Phase 1: Pre-Game & Event Launch (0 – 10 Mins)
1. **Access Control Room**: Navigate to `https://www.cantonquests.com/admin/live` (or `/admin/live` locally).
2. **Set Event Phase**: Click **OPENING** on the Phase Selector Bar.
3. **Broadcast Launch Announcement**:
   - Click **📢 Broadcast Alert**.
   - **Headline**: `🎉 CANTON QUEST WEEKEND IS LIVE!`
   - **Message**: `Welcome Agents! Stand by for live flash drops, secret passcode broadcasts, and roaming NPC hints.`
   - **Urgency**: `Urgent Broadcast`

### Phase 2: First Field Wave & Roaming NPC (10 – 30 Mins)
1. **Position Roaming NPC**:
   - Click **🕵️ Update Roaming NPC**.
   - **Zone**: `4th Street Arts Corridor`
   - **Clue**: `Spotted near Aura Craft Coffee carrying secret cards.`
2. **Broadcast Passcode Drop**:
   - Click **🔑 Secret Passcode**.
   - **Passcode**: `FOUNDER2026` (+150 XP)
   - Broadcast to players via chat/announcement or physical cards.

### Phase 3: Pop-Up Flash Drop & Bonus Sprint (30 – 50 Mins)
1. **Launch Pop-Up Flash Drop**:
   - Click **⚡ Flash Drop**.
   - Select `Market Square Signal Drop` (or `Palace Marquee Flash`).
   - Set duration to `30 Minutes`.
2. **Activate Category Double XP**:
   - Click **🔥 Bonus Window**.
   - **Multiplier**: `2.0x Double XP`
   - **Category**: `Puzzle Quests`
   - **Duration**: `45 Minutes`

### Phase 4: Score Audit & Crowd Objective Unlock (50 – 70 Mins)
1. **Inspect Field Activity**:
   - Monitor the **Real-Time Field Activity Stream** and **Leaderboard**.
   - Review pending media proofs (photos/videos) in `/admin`.
2. **Manual Field Award**:
   - If an agent completes an impressive creative pose, click **➕ Adjust Score**.
   - Award `+100 XP` with audit reason: `Creative Excellence Bonus`.

### Phase 5: Grand Finale Mode (70 – 90 Mins)
1. **Grant Wildcard Qualifications**:
   - Click **👑 Grant Wildcard** for top solo agents or squad leaders.
2. **Trigger Finale Mode**:
   - Click **🏆 Trigger Finale Mode** (sets active phase to `FINALE`).
   - Broadcast alert: `🏆 GRAND FINALE IS ACTIVE — Head to Centennial Plaza!`
3. **Conclude & Award Prizes**:
   - Inspect the final standings on the Squad and Individual Leaderboards.
   - Record winner prize assignments in `/admin`.

---

## 🛠️ Emergency Controls
- **Emergency Event Pause**: If severe weather, traffic issues, or location closures occur, click **🛑 EMERGENCY PAUSE**. All submission timers lock safely without data loss.
- **Score Ledger Reconciliation**: Click **🔄 Reconcile Scores** anytime to audit and recalculate total player XP directly from the immutable score ledger.
