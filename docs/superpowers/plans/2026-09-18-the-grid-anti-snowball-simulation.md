# Grid Anti-Snowball Simulation

## Goal

Exercise the existing Phase 5 counterweights together without inventing production tuning values. The simulation is config-driven and can be rerun against any future Canton or City #002 balance package.

## Mechanical checks

- Sweep a leader from zero territories through full city control and verify every configured Dominance Heat counter-pressure effect is non-decreasing.
- Verify at least one Dominance Heat counter-pressure actually increases as control concentrates.
- Max each City Power component individually and prove a one-axis strategy cannot reach total City Power when the configuration caps individual component weight below 100%.
- Create two otherwise-equal Surge hotspot candidates and verify positive underdog weighting ranks the underdog opportunity first.
- Verify Surge never reduces dominance exposure below regular-season baseline.

`guardrailsEngage=true` means those mechanical counterweights are configured and functioning together. It does not claim the season is perfectly balanced or predict player outcomes; full balance still requires repeated simulation campaigns using approved production tuning.
