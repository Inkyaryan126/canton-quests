# Canton Quests — World Building & City Mythology Framework

---

## 1. Concept: Canton as a Living Game World

Canton Quests transforms the physical city of Canton, Ohio into a living narrative canvas. Rather than presenting generic tasks, quests are woven into an evolving urban mythos where historical facts blend seamlessly with mysterious fictional lore.

### 2.1 Recurring Symbols & Glyphs
- **The Canton Compass**: A distinctive geometric symbol embedded in physical stickers, hidden decals, app icons, and physical pins throughout the city.
- **Cipher Markings**: Subtle visual runes located on partner storefront windows or public murals that hint at secret quest chains.

### 2.2 Hidden Factions (Conceptual Alignment)
Players can align or discover lore related to fictional, playful city factions during seasonal arcs:
- **The Guild of Architects**: Guardians of Canton's historic landmarks, brick roads, and industrial heritage.
- **The Cipher Society**: Cryptographers and puzzle masters operating out of downtown coffee shops and libraries.
- **The Night Watch**: Explorers who specialize in dusk and evening flash quests.
- **The Spark Syndicate**: Innovators, artists, and local creators behind Canton's vibrant arts scene.

### 2.3 Mysterious Characters & NPCs
- **Live Actors / Admin NPCs**: During major weekend events, real-world actors or staff dressed in character (e.g. "The Courier", "The Clockmaker") appear at specific Canton locations for timed interactions.
- **Digital Contacts**: SMS/Chat-based NPCs (e.g. "Agent 330") who send automated cryptic tips to teams who solve specific cipher chains.

### 2.4 Seasonal Story Arcs
Every 3–4 months, Canton Quests launches a new **Volume / Season**:
- **Volume 1**: *The Founder's Cipher* (Focus on downtown history, hidden symbols, and civic secrets).
- **Volume 2**: *The Industrial Catalyst* (Focus on Canton's manufacturing legacy, steam tunnels, and vintage machinery lore).
- **Volume 3**: *The Hall of Shadows* (Focus on sports heritage, stadium legends, and secret champions).

### 2.5 Secret Locations & Easter Eggs
- **Phantom Checkpoints**: Unmarked locations in Canton that do not appear on the map until a player enters within 15 meters or solves a prerequisite riddle.
- **Physical Easter Eggs**: Custom engraved brass tokens or hidden physical micro-sculptures installed with permission at partner venues.

### 2.6 Collectibles & Artifacts
- **Digital Keepsakes**: 3D-rendered digital artifacts awarded upon completing narrative storylines (e.g. "The McKinley Key", "The Hall Medallion").
- **Physical Souvenir Tokens**: Collectible enamel pins or brass coins earned during event finales.

---

## 3. World-Building Design Rules

1. **Respect Real History**: Always honor real historical figures and communities in Canton. Fictional lore supplements, but never distorts or disrespects, actual history.
2. **Leave Blank Spaces**: Do not overwrite the whole story in Phase 0. Allow player actions and community theories during Season 1 to influence future lore expansions.
3. **Immersive Tone**: App copy and push notifications should sound like an urban adventure system, never corporate or bureaucratic.
