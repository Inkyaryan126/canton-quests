# Canton Quests — Business Model & Monetization Strategy

---

## 1. Core Principle: Absolute Anti-Pay-To-Win

> **Monetization in Canton Quests must NEVER allow players to purchase points, buy leaderboard ranks, or pay for automatic quest completions.**

All purchases are strictly restricted to:
- Event entry passes / tickets
- Premium aesthetic collectibles & physical merchandise
- Special non-competitive date-night / corporate packages
- Local business promotions & discounts

---

## 2. Value Proposition Matrix

```
                    ┌───────────────────────────────┐
                    │    FOUR-WAY VALUE HARMONY     │
                    └───────────────┬───────────────┘
                                    │
       ┌────────────────┬───────────┴───────────┬────────────────┐
       ▼                ▼                       ▼                ▼
 [PLAYER VALUE]  [BUSINESS VALUE]        [SPONSOR VALUE]   [TOURISM VALUE]
```

### 2.1 Player Value
- Thrilling real-world entertainment, community connection, city discovery, exclusive local perks, and memorable social adventures.

### 2.2 Local Business Value
- Foot traffic directly inside storefronts, brand exposure to highly engaged locals/tourists, customer acquisition during off-peak hours (e.g. Saturday afternoon).

### 2.3 Sponsor Value
- Premium brand activation, custom co-branded sponsored quest chains, high-visibility digital/physical placements, and direct foot-traffic engagement metrics.

### 2.4 City / Tourism Value
- Increased downtown foot traffic, activation of civic assets and parks, positive local press, and weekend economic activity for Canton.

---

## 3. Potential Revenue Streams

### 3.1 Event Passes & Ticket Tiers
- **Free Basic Pass**: Access to core weekend event quests, main leaderboard, and digital badges (ensures broad community participation).
- **VIP Event Pass ($15–$25)**: Includes official event physical pin, souvenir map pack, partner discount booklet, and access to exclusive post-event finale party.

### 3.2 Local Business Partner Packages
- **Quest Location Partner ($150–$300 / Event)**: Storefront featured as a quest node; guaranteed foot-traffic scan objective.
- **Featured Quest Sponsor ($500+ / Event)**: Custom branded quest (e.g. "The Coffee Roaster's Secret Recipe") with special passphrase requirement at counter.

### 3.3 Major Corporate & Title Sponsors
- **Weekend Event Title Sponsor**: Overall event co-branding (e.g. *"Canton Quests: The Founder's Cipher — Presented by Local Bank"*).
- **Prize Pool Sponsorship**: Cash or product prizes provided by sponsors in exchange for trophy presentation rights at the finale.

### 3.4 Private / Custom Quests
- **Corporate Team Building**: Custom private quest events built for Canton employers and team outings.
- **Birthday & Date-Night Packages**: Self-guided small-scale quest packs available year-round for private groups.

### 3.5 Merchandise & Physical Collectibles
- Official Canton Quests t-shirts, hoodies, enamel pins, physical cipher wheels, and souvenir field books.

---

## 4. Sponsor Integrity Guidelines

1. **No Intrusive Ads**: Banner ads or pop-ups that interrupt active gameplay are prohibited.
2. **Organic Integration**: Sponsored quests must be naturally fun and contextual (e.g. discovering a secret detail inside a vintage bookstore), not an awkward sales pitch.
3. **Always Free Alternative**: If a quest step involves a business, players are never forced to buy goods to receive verification.
