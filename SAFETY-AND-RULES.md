# Canton Quests — Safety Protocols, Code of Conduct & Rules

---

## 1. Safety Directive: Zero Stupid & Zero Illegal Behavior

> **No quest, prize, or points value is worth injury, property damage, or illegal conduct. Safety is built into quest design, not bolted on as a legal disclaimer.**

Canton Quests encourages real-world urban exploration, but strictly prohibits any activity that endangers players, the public, or private property.

---

## 2. Core Safety Protocols

### 2.1 Trespassing & Property Rights
- **Public Domain First**: All physical quest objectives must be situated in publicly accessible areas (public parks, sidewalks, plazas) or inside participating partner businesses during official operating hours.
- **Strict Boundary Rules**: Residential yards, restricted utility corridors, construction sites, railway tracks, and private alleyways are **100% OFF-LIMITS**.
- **No Physical Alterations**: Players must never dig, climb trees, dismantle property, or tamper with fixtures to find clues.

### 2.2 Traffic & Pedestrian Safety
- **Crosswalk Compliance**: Players must obey all traffic signals and cross streets strictly within marked crosswalks.
- **No Drive-By Gameplay**: Mobile app interface locks out verification buttons if moving faster than 15 mph (24 km/h) to prevent drivers from using the app while operating vehicles.

### 2.3 Nighttime Gameplay Rules
- **Curfew Windows**: Outdoor public park quests automatically deactivate at dusk (or 9:00 PM).
- **Illuminated Zones Only**: Any quest active after 8:00 PM must be located in well-lit downtown districts with high public visibility and police patrol presence.

### 2.4 Protection of Minors
- **Under-18 Players**: Players under the age of 18 must be accompanied by a parent/legal guardian or participate as part of a registered adult-led team.
- **Privacy First**: Photos featuring facial views of minors are never published publicly without explicit parental consent.

### 2.5 Alcohol & Licensed Establishments
- **Optional Partner Venues**: Quests located near or inside breweries, bars, or restaurants are strictly optional and must have a non-alcohol alternative pathway.
- **Intoxication Policy**: Players exhibiting disruptive or intoxicated behavior will be immediately disqualified and removed from the active event leaderboard.

---

## 3. Player Code of Conduct & Anti-Harassment

1. **Respect the Community**: Be courteous to Canton residents, fellow competitors, business employees, and city officials.
2. **Zero Harassment**: Intimidation, aggressive behavior, following other teams, or obstructing competitors will result in instant lifetime bans.
3. **Leave No Trace**: Clean up trash, respect public art, and leave Canton cleaner than you found it.

---

## 4. Anti-Cheat & Proof Integrity

- **Original Submissions Only**: Submitting stock photos, AI-generated images, or pre-recorded videos will result in team disqualification.
- **Location Spoofing Guard**: App validates GPS accuracy, cellular tower data, and device hardware signatures to block spoofed location tools.
- **Passphrase Leaks**: Sharing secret passphrases on public social media during an active event window voids points earned for that quest.

---

## 5. Prohibited Quest Categories

The following quest concepts are permanently banned from Canton Quests:
- ❌ Climbing buildings, rooftops, bridges, or retaining walls over 3 feet tall.
- ❌ Entering bodies of water (rivers, lakes, fountains).
- ❌ Reaching into dark unlit cavities, drain pipes, or electrical boxes.
- ❌ Any task requiring physical speed across active roadways.
- ❌ Anything requiring consumption of unknown food or liquids.

---

## 6. Emergency & Incident Response

- **In-App Emergency Button**: Every screen includes an immediate "Report Incident / Emergency" button that pauses the team's timer and displays 911 contact details alongside exact GPS coordinates.
- **On-Call Event Safety Marshals**: Live staff monitor active event zones in Canton throughout active weekend hours.
