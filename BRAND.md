# Canton Quests — Brand Identity & Visual Guidelines

---

## 1. Brand Pillars & Feel

Canton Quests visual and verbal identity is defined by four core feelings:

```
[MYSTERIOUS & URBAN]  +  [EXCITING & ADVENTUROUS]  +  [PLAYFUL & UNDERGROUND]  +  [MODERN & MEMORABLE]
```

### What We ARE
- **Mysterious & Urban**: Sleek dark modes, glowing neon accents, tactile street elements, high-tech field scanner interfaces.
- **Exciting & Adventurous**: High energy, sense of discovery, pulse-panning countdowns, real-world triumph.
- **Playful & Underground**: Secret passphrases, hidden factions, cryptic glyphs, insider community vibe.
- **Modern & Memorable**: Clean typography, crisp vector graphics, instant PWA performance.

### What We ARE NOT (Anti-Aesthetics to Avoid)
- ❌ **NOT Corporate SaaS**: No sterile white enterprise dashboards, blue primary buttons, or generic office vector illustrations.
- ❌ **NOT Generic Escape Room**: No tacky blood-splattered skulls, fake spiderwebs, or cheesy haunted house tropes.
- ❌ **NOT Childish Treasure Maps**: No burnt-edge parchment paper, pirate X-marks-the-spot maps, or cartoon parrots.
- ❌ **NOT Overly Serious Military / Tactical**: No camouflage background patterns, stencil stencil fonts, or aggressive military jargon.

## 2. Voice & Tone

| Context | Tone | Example Copy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Headline / Teaser** | Cryptic, Inviting, Energetic | *"Canton is hiding something. Bring a team and find out what."* |
| **Quest Unlock** | Adventurous, Clear | *"Signal detected near Centennial Plaza. Solve the cipher before 4:00 PM."* |
| **Verification Success** | Celebratory, High-Octane | *"Cipher Cracked! +350 XP awarded to Team Apex."* |
| **Error / Invalid Code** | Helpful, Playful | *"Signal lost. That code doesn't match this frequency. Re-examine the plaque closely."* |
| **Safety Warning** | Firm, Uncompromising | *"Agent Safety Rule: Never cross active roadways outside marked crosswalks."* |

## 3. Visual System Recommendations (Non-Binding Guidance)

### 3.1 Color Palette Philosophy
- **Primary Background**: Deep Obsidian / Midnight Black (`#0B0F17`)
- **Card / Surface**: Dark Slate / Carbon (`#161E2E`)
- **Accent Glow 1**: Neon Electric Amber (`#F59E0B` / `#FBBF24`)
- **Accent Glow 2**: Cyber Cyan / Emerald (`#10B981` / `#06B6D4`)
- **Text Primary**: Crisp Ivory / White (`#F9FAFB`)
- **Text Secondary**: Muted Steel Gray (`#9CA3AF`)

### 3.2 Typography Direction
- **Headings & Display**: Modern geometric sans-serif with bold industrial weight (e.g. *Outfit*, *Space Grotesk*, or *Syne*).
- **Body & Controls**: Clean, highly readable interface sans-serif (e.g. *Inter* or *Plus Jakarta Sans*).
- **Monospace / Codes**: Crisp tech monospace for coordinates, timer badges, and ciphers (e.g. *JetBrains Mono* or *Fira Code*).

---

## 4. Logo & Iconography Guidelines

- Logo consists of a sleek, geometric compass emblem merged with a location pin and cipher ring.
- Use iconography that feels like a modern field radar tool rather than standard web app icons.
